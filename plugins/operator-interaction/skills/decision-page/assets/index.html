For each decision, pick one option or one follow-up (the radio buttons allow only one), and add your words if you like. Each click saves at once; there is no submit button. Tell Claude in chat when you're done. The map under the progress bar lists the groups; each decision is one line in its group, and a click on the line opens its card (Expand all and Collapse all open or close every card). Use recommendation and Later beside a line answer without opening the card. A number · name is a decision: hover over it or focus it to see it, and click it to open its card; on one not on this page, a tap or click pins what it shows, and a second tap, a tap elsewhere or Escape closes it. Each line shows, after an arrow (→) and its letter, what the recommended option changes in the system the work builds (with no recommendation, each option's, in a few words); each card opens on a TLDR, then its Context (the terms it uses), then its impact, one facet a line, then one line per option; the folds under it go from compressed to full detail. A part with a more detail button, above or inside a fold, shows more detail as short bullets when you click it or its text, and full detail in headed sections on a second click; More beside an option shows it in full, with its own effect, reach, undo and cost. A part with nothing to say (none) is left out. An Impact describes the system the work builds, never the review or the process; its later: line says what waiting holds up. Open questions, folded above the Impact, lists what is not known yet.
