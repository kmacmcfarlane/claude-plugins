# Rulings

The operator has ruled on these, except the parts marked as not theirs; each keeps the
alternative not taken, in case practice argues for it.

- **Labelled exceptions** (2026-09-24) — the labels in SKILL.md § The floor. Not taken: no exceptions at all.
- **Basis word** (2026-09-24) — `strong | partial | thin | none` from the weakest load-bearing
  claim. Not taken: a confidence bucket; a step-down arithmetic over every tag.
- **No confidence percentage** (2026-09-24), shown or used for ordering. Not taken: a
  calibrated number used internally.
- **Defaults** (2026-09-24) — status-quo only; no timed action defaults. Not taken: timed
  defaults for reversible, narrow decisions.
- **Default wake** (2026-09-24) — the next time you finish a piece of work and report. Not
  taken: the next operator turn after one other exchange; events only.
- **Deadlines first** (2026-09-24) — before the operator's known return, else every stated
  deadline. Not taken: a 4-hour "likely back" window.
- **Read-back scope** (2026-09-24) — only when the chosen option is one-way. Not taken: every
  answer on a ⚠ decision.
- **⚠ blocks** (2026-09-24) — on first show and when cold. Not taken: a block in every message.
- **Placement** (2026-09-24) — decisions last, the list and hint at the tail. Not taken: the
  list first.
- **Seen** (2026-09-24) — the operator has taken a turn since it was shown. Not taken: rendered
  once.
- **Hint scope** — messages with two or more decisions. Not taken: every message that asks
  for a decision.
- **Option order** (2026-09-29) — (a), (b), (c) … (z), the recommended option in bold in
  its own place. Not taken: the recommended option moved first, as a modal dialog lists it.
- **A recommendation on every decision** (2026-09-29) — shown on the decision's own line
  and card, never folded into a line or card shared with others. Not taken: one grouped
  entry for related decisions, listing their recommendations together.
- **Round asks justify themselves** (2026-09-29) — what the leftover findings would break,
  and what the round costs in time, quota and attention. Not taken: a bare waiver ask whose
  template carries the floor by reference.
- **Every ask justifies itself** (2026-09-30) — *why ask* on every decision, opening with its
  class when the caller names classes. Not taken: the justification on round asks only.
- **Resume cue** (2026-09-30; shown on every card and block since 2026-10-12, folded into
  the Context section below) — a **Context:** cue stored when raised, shown to a cold reader
  under *while it waited*. Not taken: every cold decision as a block.
- **Shown after** (2026-09-30) — what a caller's rules let you decide alone is recorded and
  shown as a `Done:` line in a *Done alone* group, never left unseen. Not taken: a record seen
  only on request.
- **Impact in every view** (2026-10-06; Undo only where an option is one-way or costly since
  2026-10-20, below) — five facets in one order, the smallest view
  showing the effect, each larger view adding facets; the Impact line stored at raise and
  read by every view; Undo replaces the stakes words, Wait the *blocks* slot. Not taken:
  lines with the effect only, no wait; no stored line, each view deriving impact from the
  options each time.
- **Shown record** (2026-10-07) — the first and the last showing of each decision are
  recorded, with the turn that sees each, written by the session that displays it. Not
  taken: the last showing only (last-write-wins).
- **Plain integers** (2026-10-08) — every decision, sub-decisions and follow-up decisions
  included, its own plain integer. Not taken: lettered or dotted sub-numbers (`8b`, `8.1`).
- **Inputs to act** (2026-10-06, the operator's ask; a part of its own, named
  **To act on (x):** on the card, `act` on an answer page and `to act (x):` in a store: the
  operator's answer of 2026-10-08; where the part sits and what each step holds: a design
  made in review, not the operator's) — everything an option the operator carries out needs,
  on the card. Not taken: inputs sent after the answer; plainer names (**Steps for (x):**);
  the inputs written into each option's own text.
- **Terms defined where shown** (2026-10-07, the operator's ask; words over labels is a
  design made in review, not the operator's; a source label goes first with its short name
  since 2026-10-20, below) — every term the operator may not know,
  defined where it first appears. Not taken: a pointer to the source.
- **Context first on an answer page** (order since 2026-10-09: TLDR first) (2026-10-08, the
  operator's direction; the checks' shape, a design made in planning, not the operator's) —
  every page card opens on a flat Context introducing the terms, items and concepts its
  Impact line, TLDR and options use, and its TLDR carries no recommendation, which the options
  mark. Not taken: definitions left to What in the Background fold; a Rec bullet in the TLDR.
  Background not auto-expanded; Context absorbs What (decision 197 (a), the operator's answer
  2026-10-08). Not taken: Background open on load.
- **Block depth on an answer page, by detail level** (levels optional since 201) (2026-10-08,
  decision 198, the operator's (a) with changes, and the operator's label **More** for the
  option button; the field shape, the sizes for the visible parts' levels and the lints,
  designs made in planning, not the operator's) — every page card carries the decisions
  block's content at soft sizes, read at its full level (Background ~150 words, each option
  ~60–120, Evidence ~150 with paths and links, a card ~600–900). The visible parts step
  summary → medium → full on a click; a part inside an opened fold may step the same way; the
  folds stay folded; **More** beside an option shows it in full. Not taken: the folds'
  content moved into the visible parts; depth only on ⚠ and wide cards; no sizes.
- **Detail levels scale with the decision** (2026-10-09, decision 201 (c), the operator's
  answer: "I want the levels to scale a bit with the size of the decision") — levels are
  optional on every part of a page card: a small call may carry none; a wide or one-way
  decision carries the most. The pre-publish runner lints thin depth only where a part has
  levels. Not taken: visible levels expected on every card; small calls exempt by a mark.
- **TLDR first on an answer page** (2026-10-09, the operator's direction) — the open card and
  the popup show the TLDR, then Context, then the Impact. Context still introduces the terms
  the card uses, and no longer restates what the TLDR decides. Not taken: Context first
  (2026-10-08).
- **Level shapes on an answer page** (2026-10-09, the operator's direction; the data shape,
  the lint thresholds and the cap of two sub-bullets a bullet, grouping the options where a
  facet differs across more than two, are designs made in planning, not the operator's) —
  each level clearly sparser than the next: a summary one or two sentences (the TLDR one or
  two bullets); medium a list of bullets with an optional sub-bullet or two; high headed
  sections with bullets and sub-bullets; terse sentence fragments at summary and medium. Not
  taken: levels as fuller renditions in the same prose shape (198, as built).
- **No ids on an answer page** (2026-10-09, the operator's direction) — short names, titles and
  slugs only, page and layer titles included: the page is the reader's only context. Not
  taken: plain words first, the id after (the page's rule from 2026-10-08). The plain-names
  skill's chat rule stands.
- **Impact across the options on an answer page** (2026-10-09, the operator's direction) — the
  card-level Impact covers the decision across all its options, each facet on its own line;
  each option's effect lives in its own detail. Not taken: Effect as the recommended option's
  on a page.
- **Effect across the options in chat, and each option's own impact** (2026-10-14; each
  option's undo only where it is one-way or costly since 2026-10-20, below; answer
  206 (a), widened by the operator: "is there a provision for displaying the
  effect/reach/undo/cost in TUI cards/blocks per-option too? That parity seems valuable.
  Make it so"; the italic line under each option, facets that do not apply left out, and
  older stored lines read as written, designs made in build, not the operator's) — the
  Impact line's Effect, stored and shown, covers the decision across all its options,
  favouring none, as the page's does; each option on a card carries its own effect, reach,
  undo and cost on an italic line under it, and a block shows the card's Impact line above
  its table. Not taken: Effect as the recommended option's in chat (206 (b)); per-option
  facets on the list line. The list line keeping the recommended option's own effect after
  its label (a build design) is pending the operator's answer to decision 219.
- **A Context section on chat cards and blocks** (2026-10-12, the operator's direction: "the
  decision skill's TUI output should have a context section for cards and blocks, similar to
  how the decision-page works"; the cue folded in as Context's last clause, the What line
  doing the TLDR's work, the sizes (a block at the page's medium only, high staying a
  page-only level), the origin clause for a subject never seen (*raised when …*, worded to
  stay true after it is seen), older stored cards with no Context keeping the old order, and
  the stored line's reading, designs made in build, not the operator's) — every card and
  block in chat carries a **Context:** section shaped like the page's `context`, in the
  page's order: the decision line, then Context, then the Impact and the options; a card's
  at the page's summary size, a block's at its medium; written when raised, stored with the
  card, shown every time; a list line carries none. Not taken: the cue alone, shown to a
  cold reader only (2026-09-30); the cue on a line of its own above Context; a block's
  Context at the page's high size.
- **No Options in full; none is not shown, on an answer page** (2026-10-14, the operator's
  direction: "the options in full section is redundant to the inline details in each option,
  ditch it. If any sub-section is empty or none, just don't display it"; the required Impact
  facets, each option's Undo cell, the basis word and the rec line's *unknown: none* kept as
  statements, (z) given a
  **More**, and a one-way card's options shown open, designs made in build, not the
  operator's) — each option's **More** carries what the fold did (its text at every level,
  its impact and its row); a part, facet or field that is empty or none is left off the page.
  Not taken: the fold kept beside **More** (198, as built); *none* written out.
- **Impact names the system** (2026-10-20, answer 259 (a), from the operator's review
  feedback: "why [is] the impact of this decision scoped to the impact to my code review,
  not the system we are building? that's silly", and "the decision skill has described the
  change or process instead of the impact on the codebase and downstream consumers"; the
  reading that sessions and the operator are the users where the change is to agent
  behaviour, a design made in planning, and that the operator's time to carry out an option
  stays in Cost, a design made in build; neither the operator's) — Effect and Reach describe the thing the work builds:
  its code, data, users, deploys and downstream consumers. An effect on the process — a held
  review comment, a round, a blocked session — goes only in Wait. Cost keeps build time and
  spend, and the operator's attention only on a round ask. Not taken: a process clause in
  Effect when the process is all the decision changes (259 (b)).
- **Undo only where an option is one-way or costly** (2026-10-20, answer 260 (a): "we
  shouldn't mention that the impact is reversible, only mention when it ISN'T, since things
  are mostly reversible without consequence ... it should be attention-getting when it needs
  to be"; the `⚠ undo:` marker and the answer page's `oneway` row flag were asked on the
  2026-10-16 card as separate recommended parts, OQ2 (i) and (p), and are the plan's
  sub-parts adopted with (a); the block's `—` cell and its dropped column, and older stored
  undos read as written, designs made in planning, not the operator's) — undo is shown only
  on an option that is one-way or costly to undo, marked `⚠ undo:`; an option with none
  shown is reversible at little cost, and the read-back reads it that way. The Impact line
  carries undo only when some option is one-way or costly, naming it. The marker marks the
  option, never the decision: batches, `you decide` and the ⚠ block go by the decision's
  own `⚠ one-way` label. This reverses *Impact in every view* (2026-10-06), which put undo
  on every Impact line, and answer 206 (2026-10-14), which put each option's undo on its
  line and every option's on a ⚠ decision. Not taken: every option of a ⚠ one-way decision
  keeping its undo (260 (b)); undo kept everywhere (260 (c)); a bold `undo:` with no icon
  (OQ2 (ii)); no page row flag, the cold read the only check (OQ2 (q)).
- **Why you, why now: one line** (2026-10-20, answer 261 (a), from the operator's words:
  "Background (why ask and why now?) are more metacognitive than necessary"; the length of
  about 25 words, its place above the Rec line, and the block keeping two lines, designs
  made in planning, not the operator's) — on a chat card, why now and why ask share one
  **Why you, why now:** line, the class first, below the options and just above the Rec
  line; what the decision blocks is left to Wait. The answer page keeps them folded in
  Background, and a store keeps them as two fields. *Every ask justifies itself*
  (2026-09-30) stands: every card still says why it is asked. Not taken: one short line
  each, where they were (261 (b)); dropping them unless the reason is not obvious (261 (c)),
  which would have reversed it.
- **Bundles as groups of whole item cards** (2026-10-20, answer 263 (a), from the operator's
  words on a card that bundled four findings: "I'd expect impact for each of these findings
  in the impact section ... Without more info on what I'm actually deciding on, I can't
  decide"; the heading's form and its **Shared context:** line, designs made in planning,
  not the operator's) — when a bundle's items need different answers, each is its own whole
  card, with its own number, Impact, options and recommendation, under one group heading
  that adds the shared context; `ok N-M` still takes every recommendation. When one answer
  fits every item, it stays one card naming each. It applies *A recommendation on every
  decision* (2026-09-29) and *Plain integers* (2026-10-08). Not taken: one card with a pick
  per item (263 (b)), which would have reversed both; always one answer for all (263 (c)).
- **Explain the mechanism** (2026-10-20, answer 268 (a), from the operator's words on a
  finding labelled "exposes an archived library in its public API": "How do importers break?
  what are the consequences?") — every effect, finding and risk says how it happens, who
  meets it and when, and the before and after of the fix; the cold read checks it. Not
  taken: the rule on findings and risk cards only (268 (b)).
- **Source labels first, with a short name** (2026-10-20, answer 269 (a), widened: "Be sure
  to expand Fn style labels (in all forms, not just starting with "F") to include a

  none, label first: *F7 (oversize upload)*. Ticket keys and other tracker ids are not
  source labels and are excluded: they keep the `plain-names` skill's rule. A decision

  *Plain integers* (2026-10-08); the label trailing, as before (269 (d)); the slug form of
